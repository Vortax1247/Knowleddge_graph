# Public roadmap

This roadmap describes gates, not promised dates.

## Stage 1 - Reliability

- complete the governed V4.1 endurance window;
- review service latency and availability;
- preserve the protected-file drift guarantee.

## Stage 2 - V5 candidate

- generate candidate cases without opening the frozen holdout;
- perform human review of the training material;
- train an isolated candidate;
- compare candidate and control on independent evidence.

## Stage 3 - Release decision

- require deterministic and neural contracts;
- conduct blind human evaluation;
- verify canary behavior and rollback;
- promote only after explicit owner approval.

## Stage 4 - Isolated cognitive states

- verify memory isolation;
- complete the owner-approved legacy transfer;
- keep Dirigeant, Banquier and Ami inactive until every gate passes;
- retain a shared engine without shared private memory.

## Non-goal

The roadmap does not target unsupervised autonomy. It targets useful delegation with observable boundaries and human authority.
